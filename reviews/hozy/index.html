A Relaxing Simulator for Designing Diverse Rooms.
Many might compare Hozy to The Sims series or casual mobile titles, but it operates on a completely different level. The game imposes no strict rules: it simply hands you various spaces to arrange, each with its own tiny background story and unique owners. The routine of handling furniture and personal belongings feels so thoughtfully reimagined that even sweeping floors or picking up trash becomes genuinely satisfying. However, the game is quite short and can easily be completed in a single long evening session. You might also encounter a few task tracking bugs — sometimes a objective gets marked as completed before you even touch it, while other times a fully cleaned spot refuses to register. Still, the core mechanics are executed brilliantly. Sweeping fallen leaves never feels tedious, the controls are responsive, and the visual presentation is solid; even on low graphics settings, the game retains a distinct and cozy atmospheric style. That said, the system requirements feel slightly higher than expected for a project of this scale.

At its core, Hozy is a simulator centered around cleaning and fulfilling your interior design ideas. It serves as a perfect playground for perfectionists, allowing you to drag and drop hangers, furniture, toys, and various decor items absolutely anywhere. The game equally accommodates neat structure and absolute chaos.

The standout feature is undoubtedly the level design. There are no generic or dull houses here; every space feels distinct. The architecture of each room is carefully crafted, completely avoiding repetitive, square boxy layouts in favor of interesting geometry and spatial depth.

The gameplay loop is equally engaging. You are presented with a linear progression of houses that unlock one after another. Before diving in, it is best to inspect the area, assess the workload, and read through the task list. Following the objectives in order is highly recommended, as tackling them haphazardly can trigger minor bugs that disrupt the cozy mood.

Verdict:

A great retreat from heavy, high-stress games that never puts the player in an uncomfortable position. Although the amount of content and houses is modest, this relaxing experiment easily delivers two to three hours of enjoyment. The only real downside is the surprisingly high hardware demands, which feel somewhat unoptimized for a title of this scope. It may not be a masterpiece, but it is precisely the kind of comforting game many players need right now.

Your main tasks involve wiping floors, washing windows, painting walls, and placing furniture. Occasionally, you will also clear out trash, replace wooden planks in a treehouse, or lay down tiles inside a lighthouse. For wall painting, you can choose from three color palettes.

The real magic, however, lies in item placement. Hozy distinctly divides the process into two phases: renovation first, interior decoration second. The interactivity is impressive — before placing items, you must unpack them from cardboard boxes categorized by type (seating, tables, small decor, lighting, and toys). Unboxing each new item constantly reshapes your layout plans on the fly. Accompanying the gameplay is a soft, melodic soundtrack that fits the chill atmosphere perfectly.
