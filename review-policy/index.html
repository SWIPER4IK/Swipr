How Do Ratings Work?

Score (0.0–100.0): A 100-point rating scale with decimals, designed to reflect a game’s variety, mechanics, polish, atmosphere, execution, and overall value. Rather than judging elements in isolation, our methodology evaluates how effectively every system interconnects to create a cohesive whole.

Recommendation (Yes / No): This is a personal binary verdict. A game can receive a decent score for its production design (e.g., 60-70/100), but still not be recommended if it fails to engage its audience or suffers from tedious pacing.

What are featured reviews?

Featured Reviews represent our flagship coverage, ordered from highest to lowest score.

What are upcoming reviews?

Upcoming Reviews highlight games currently in coverage, slated for publication in the near future. Release windows for certain reviews may shift due to unforeseen circumstances.
